# Art direction and production record

Original production artwork generated for Super Duper Jaydens! with the image-generation tool. No franchise stills, characters, logos, or soundtrack samples were used as assets. Production interviews were consulted for broad historical techniques, not licensed imagery.

## Direction

- Black-paper-style painted city backgrounds, strong architectural perspective, confident cel outlines and hard shadow shapes.
- Midnight navy, black, burgundy sky, warm amber light, restrained teal edges.
- Natural young-hero proportions, not chibi or geometric icon people.
- Bolt’s active claw remains on the anatomical left shoulder. Front three-quarter views place it on the viewer’s right. No mirrored character images. Two scene poses were repaired against the approved cover reference.
- Artwork is displayed directly without SVG displacement filters or pixelated placeholder upscaling. WebP sizes are optimized; the approved cover has a smaller responsive source.
