# Curriculum and license audit

Reviewed November 20, 2026. Super Duper Jaydens! is an independent, original educational game. Stories, models, artwork, interfaces, and recordings were created for this project. Early-reader language and examples replace academic prose; the concepts are reordered into thirteen fictional superhero missions. No textbook paragraphs, diagrams, videos, proprietary worksheets, or quotations are reproduced as game assets. The sources are not endorsements.

## Japanese influence

The game introduces visible work, work-in-progress limits, and small iterative improvements. The term kaizen is explicitly identified as Japanese. It does not present a single “Asian management style,” apply cultural stereotypes, or claim a full Lean, kanban, or Toyota Production System implementation. Manufacturing kanban can signal replenishment; a three-column board is only one simplified teaching interface.

[Toyota’s own history and explanation](https://global.toyota/en/company/vision-and-philosophy/production-system/) was consulted for context on making work easier, flow, and incremental improvement. No Toyota prose, diagrams, brands, or other media are reproduced as game assets or treated as CC BY.

## Version 1.1.0: applied operations

Original simulations now connect planning choices to extra shifts and the rover’s first-day route. Current saved mission reports determine the result; replay replaces a report rather than accumulating penalties. Classification activities explain each card. Operational activities accept complete but strained plans, show their costs, and allow revision. These deterministic outcomes are teaching models, not empirical production forecasts.

- Issue 6: capacity, overburden (muri), and waiting.
- Issue 8: explicit start/finish and movement policies, a WIP limit, four observed board metrics, and a service target. A separate returned-card dispatch models pull replenishment with a two-pack buffer. This distinguishes manufacturing signals from a knowledge-work board.
- Issue 10: stopping abnormal output, a visible call for support (andon), cause correction, retesting, and error-proofing through a shaped guide. Letting the printer run creates rework.
- Issue 12: baseline observation, a change, two trials, comparison, and a decision to keep or revise the process. Lower motion differs from pressuring a worker.

Primary conceptual references checked November 20, 2026:

- Toyota Motor Corporation, Toyota Production System: https://global.toyota/en/company/vision-and-philosophy/production-system/ . Copyrighted reference; no prose, media, logo, or proprietary course is reproduced. Its two pillars are jidoka and just-in-time. TPS also addresses ease of work and ongoing improvement; the game does not reduce it to speed or zero inventory.

## Version 1.2.0: agency and visible results

All thirteen optional flavor detours were replaced with usable field-tool choices. Every tool has one use and a stated capability. Field jobs accept matching gear, one of two starting-gadget uses, a finite supply pack, an extra crew shift, or deferral. A ledger makes these resource choices persistent and allows alternative plans to be compared without duplicate spending.

City upgrades alter later options. District delivery routes require specific links; the map bench increases reachable stops, the workshop recovers a supply pack, and the test bay recovers one delayed shift. Players allocate the final route and see delivered versus deferred stops per district. Their starting priority signal reserves one available stop in its district. The model makes trade-offs visible; these are fictional resource rules, not industrial performance claims.

For early planning missions, a complete but flawed plan can now run. The team repairs its gaps, the repair time is recorded, and the final route changes. Results retain the player’s actual tested choices and explain each decision. Incomplete selections still require a complete plan. WIP and dependency constraints remain explicit workflow rules.
